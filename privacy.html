Privacy Policy

How Originly collects, uses and protects your information — and the controls you have over it.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Overview

Originly ("we", "us", "our") is a social discovery app that helps people connect through where their life began — including birthplace, birth hospital, birthday and hometown. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you create an Originly profile, you may choose to provide information such as your name, birthday, birthplace, birth hospital, current city, gender, photos, and any other details you add to your profile. Providing origin details such as birth hospital or exact birthday is always optional — you decide how much of your story to share.

Information collected automatically

Like most apps, Originly may collect limited technical information needed to operate the service, such as device type, app version, and general location (where you enable it) used to support distance-based discovery.

How we use your information

  • To power discovery and matching based on the filters you and other users choose (hospital, birthday, birthplace, city, distance and more).
  • To operate core features such as messaging, matches, and your profile.
  • To maintain the safety and integrity of the platform, including reviewing reports and blocks.
  • To communicate with you about your account or, if you've asked for early access, about launch updates.

Your choices and controls

You control what appears on your profile and what is used for discovery. You can adjust your visibility settings, hide specific details, report or block other users, and request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information you choose to include on your profile is visible to other Originly users according to your visibility settings. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the app, under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we are required to retain limited records for legal or safety purposes.

Children's privacy

Originly is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to use the app in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from children below that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as Originly develops. We'll update the date at the top of this page when changes are made.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at info@originlyapp.com.
